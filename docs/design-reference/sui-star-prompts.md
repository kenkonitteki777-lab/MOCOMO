# Sui star picture book — generation prompts

Built-in image generation. Six independent landscape illustrations. Reference inputs: approved Moco expressions and companion v2 (Sui only). Originals retained; web app uses WebP encoding without layout or color edits.

## Page 1

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: finished full-bleed illustration for one page of a high-quality Japanese children's picture book, landscape 3:2. Reference image 1 contains ONLY the canonical WHITE MOCOMO character, with a big soft milky-white head, three round cloud nubs (central one taller), small body, short mitten hands and feet, modest dark brown oval eyes, tiny mouth and pink cheeks. Reference image 2: use ONLY SUI, the light blue pear-shaped friend in the TOP LEFT cell, with exactly ONE tall gently curved droplet crest. No other friends. Keep these exact two distinct character identities, proportions, soft tactile finish and crowns; vary their poses, eye direction, eyebrows, mouths and body language specifically for this scene. Render them integrated naturally into the landscape, not pasted on. Dreamy world above clouds, soft cloud ground, a few delicate leaves and flowers at edges. Beautiful atmospheric depth, delicate painterly 3D softness, restrained pastel colors, warm light and emotionally legible faces. Exactly two characters, no clothes/accessories, no human, no lettering, no captions, no borders, no collage, no panels, no logos. One continuous scene. NOT identical front-facing smiles; eye direction and emotional body language must tell the scene. Medium two-shot, faces large enough for a phone, hands visible, no cropped crowns. Golden small five-point star is the recurring story object, not a reward counter.
PAGE 1 / first encounter, early afternoon. Moco stands left and Sui right on a fluffy cloud path, a little empty space between them. They cautiously notice each other. Moco tilts his head, one small mitten raised in a hesitant hello, tiny uncertain smile, eyes looking toward Sui. Sui holds own hands close, slight bashful downturned gaze then glancing toward Moco. Curiosity and gentle nervousness, not sadness. No star yet. Fresh airy sky.
